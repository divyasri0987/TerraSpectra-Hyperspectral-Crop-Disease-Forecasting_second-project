TerraSpectra: Hyperspectral Crop Disease Forecasting

1. Introduction

TerraSpectra is a Machine Learning-based project designed to detect and forecast crop diseases using hyperspectral imaging technology. Hyperspectral imaging captures detailed spectral information from crops, helping identify subtle changes in plant conditions that may indicate disease development. The project focuses on analyzing spectral patterns to understand crop health and support early disease detection.

2. Project Objective

The primary objective of TerraSpectra is to explore hyperspectral data for identifying early signs of crop diseases and developing predictive Machine Learning models. It aims to analyze spectral variations, recognize disease-related patterns, and support timely crop health monitoring.

3. Problem Statement

Traditional crop disease identification often depends on visible symptoms, which may appear after a disease has already developed. TerraSpectra focuses on using hyperspectral information to identify potential changes in crop health at an earlier stage and support proactive disease monitoring.
