Crop Disease Indicators

Crop diseases can affect plant growth, leaf structure, pigmentation, and overall health. These changes may also influence the spectral reflectance properties of plants. Understanding these indicators is important for identifying possible disease-related conditions.

Healthy vs Diseased Crop Patterns

Healthy plants generally maintain normal growth and physiological conditions, while diseased plants may show changes in their appearance and spectral characteristics. Hyperspectral data can help analyze these differences across multiple wavelength bands.

Important Spectral Features

Spectral features are useful measurements extracted from hyperspectral data. Some important characteristics include:

- Reflectance Values: Measure the amount of light reflected by plant surfaces.
- Wavelength Bands: Represent different portions of the electromagnetic spectrum.
- Vegetation Characteristics: Provide information about plant health and condition.
- Spectral Variations: Help identify differences between healthy and stressed crops.

Importance of Early Disease Identification

Early identification of crop diseases can help farmers take preventive measures before the disease spreads further. Hyperspectral imaging combined with Machine Learning can support the identification of potential disease symptoms through spectral pattern analysis.
